M5 — Transits & Themes: how the logic works
Reference notes, written before implementation. See 2026-10-26-m5-reading-checklist.md
for the build checklist and docs/spec.md section 6 for the source spec.

THE BIG PICTURE

Every day, the app looks at where the planets are RIGHT NOW ("transits") compared
to where they were when the user was born ("natal chart"), and turns that
comparison into a short list of "today's themes" - things like "good day for
admin" or "go gently on money stuff." Those themes are what:
  - the AI reading (later in M5) gets written around, and
  - task ranking (M6) uses to bump relevant to-dos up the list.

This file is about how "today's themes" actually get computed. There are two
layers: TRANSITS (pure astronomy - where are the planets today, and which
ones are forming an aspect with the user's natal planets), and THEMES (the
interpretation layer - turning those aspects into a short, ranked list of
8 possible life-area themes).

STEP 1: TRANSITS

Compute where every planet is TODAY at 12:00 noon, in the user's current
browser time zone (not their birth time zone - just "today, around midday,
wherever they are right now"). This reuses the same planet-position math
already built for the natal chart in M3.

Then compare each of today's planet positions against each of the user's
NATAL planet positions, looking for aspects (conjunction, sextile, square,
trine, opposition) - same aspect types as the natal chart, but now one side
is "today" and the other side is "the day you were born."

The orb (how close the angle has to be to count) is tighter for transits than
for the natal chart, and varies by planet:
  - Moon:                  4 degrees
  - Sun, Mercury, Venus, Mars:  3 degrees
  - Jupiter through Pluto:      2 degrees
(Moon moves fast and matters a lot day-to-day, hence the wider orb. The outer
planets move so slowly that a tight orb still catches a multi-day "window.")

Also note: is Mercury, Venus, or Mars retrograde today? (Just the 3 that
retrograde often enough to matter for daily planning - the outer planets
retrograde for months at a time, so it's not a "today" kind of signal.)

MOON PHASE

A simplified 4-phase model, based on the angle between the Sun and Moon
today (0-360 degrees):
  new:     0-45 degrees
  waxing:  45-135, and again 135-180 (both halves of "growing")
  full:    180-225 degrees
  waning:  225-360 degrees

STEP 2: PLANET -> THEMES

There are 8 "themes" (same vocabulary as task categories from M4):
  focus, communication, creative, admin, social, selfcare, money, action

Each transiting planet maps to 1-3 of them - this is what makes "Mercury is
making an aspect today" turn into "communication and admin are relevant
today":

  Sun      -> focus
  Moon     -> selfcare
  Mercury  -> communication, admin
  Venus    -> social, creative, money
  Mars     -> action, focus
  Jupiter  -> money, social
  Saturn   -> admin, focus
  Uranus   -> creative
  Neptune  -> creative, selfcare
  Pluto    -> focus

STEP 3: THE 10 STARTER RULES

Each aspect found in Step 1 gets run through these rules to build up a
"strength" score (1-3) per theme, plus a plain-English reason. The rules are
meant to be simple and tweakable later (each is its own small rule object,
not hardcoded logic), so expect these exact numbers to get adjusted after
real-world testing.

1. Harmonious aspect (trine or sextile) from a transiting planet
   -> that planet's theme(s) each gain +1 strength.
   Example: transiting Mercury trine natal Sun -> communication +1, admin +1.

2. Conjunction
   -> that planet's theme(s) gain +1 strength AND get flagged "intense"
      (shown differently in the reading/UI - a conjunction is a stronger,
      more concentrated kind of aspect than a trine/sextile).

3. Challenging aspect (square or opposition)
   -> that planet's theme(s) go into the CAUTION list instead of gaining
      strength. Caution doesn't mean "skip it" - it means "do it, but in
      smaller pieces today."

4. Any aspect (of any kind above) TO THE NATAL SUN OR MOON specifically
   -> counts DOUBLE. The Sun and Moon are the two most personally significant
      points in a chart, so an aspect landing on either one matters more than
      the same aspect landing on, say, natal Mars.
   Example: transiting Mercury trine natal MOON isn't +1 communication/admin,
   it's +2 communication/admin, because it's hitting the Moon.

5. Moon phase gives one flat bonus, regardless of aspects:
     new     -> +1 focus      (a good day to start things)
     waxing  -> +1 action
     full    -> +1 social     (also: a good day to finish and review things)
     waning  -> +1 admin      (a good day to clear the decks)

6. Mercury retrograde today -> +1 admin (review, edit, follow up on things),
   AND communication goes into caution (miscommunications are more likely).

7. Venus retrograde today -> money goes into caution.

8. Mars retrograde today -> action goes into caution, AND +1 selfcare
   (the idea being: low-energy days are a cue to rest, not push).

9. Fallback: if NO theme reaches strength 1 after all the above (a quiet,
   uneventful day astrologically), default to focus 1 and admin 1 so the
   app always has something to say.

10. Cleanup: cap every theme's strength at 3 (no matter how many rules stack
    up), keep only the TOP 3 themes overall, and keep a human-readable
    reason string for each boost (e.g. "Mercury trine your Sun") so the UI
    and the AI reading can explain WHY a theme is relevant, not just THAT
    it is.

A WORKED EXAMPLE

Say today: transiting Mercury is trine the user's natal Sun, transiting Mars
is square the user's natal Venus, the Moon is in its waning phase, and
nothing is retrograde.

  - Mercury trine natal Sun: rule 1 (harmonious, +1 communication/admin),
    then rule 4 doubles it because it's hitting the natal Sun
    -> communication +2, admin +2, reason "Mercury trine your Sun"
  - Mars square natal Venus: rule 3 (challenging) -> social and creative
    (Venus's themes) go into caution, reason "Mars square your Venus"
    (note: Mars's OWN themes - action, focus - aren't touched by this
    rule; it's Venus's themes that get the caution flag, since Venus is
    the one under challenging pressure)
  - Waning moon: rule 5 -> admin +1 more (now admin is at 3, capped)

Result: top themes are admin (3) and communication (2); caution list is
[social, creative]; moon phase "waning."

WHERE THIS FEEDS NEXT

- The AI reading (rest of M5) gets this DayThemes object as its grounding
  facts - it's not allowed to invent anything the rules didn't produce.
- Task ranking (M6) matches each task's category against these same theme
  names to bump matching tasks up the list, and softens (but doesn't hide)
  tasks whose category is in the caution list.
